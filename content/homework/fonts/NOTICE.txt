Source Han Sans CN Normal glyph fingerprint index

The SHA-256 to Unicode index in glyph-map.json is generated from the publicly
distributed SourceHanSansCN-Normal.ttf, and contains no course or user data.
Font distribution used to generate the index:
https://github.com/lincanbin/Material-Design-Avatars/blob/master/src/fonts/SourceHanSansCN-Normal.ttf
Original font project: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans

Rebuild: node tools/build-font-map.mjs <SourceHanSansCN-Normal.ttf>
Only unique matches after Unicode NFKC normalization are retained.
Unsupported or ambiguous glyphs are not guessed.
